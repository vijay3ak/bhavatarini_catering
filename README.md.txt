
# Bhavatarini Catering Service

A professional website and enquiry management platform for Bhavatarini Catering Service, based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

## Project Overview

The website will showcase traditional South Indian catering services and help customers submit catering enquiries.

The primary target customers include:
- Corporate offices
- Startups and office teams
- Small functions
- Group gatherings

## Development Principles

- Preserve the approved prototype design.
- Build reusable and maintainable components.
- Follow responsive design and accessibility standards.
- Protect application secrets.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies and paid subscriptions.
- Do not invent business information.
